    05

    How it works

    Five small, explainable steps. No black box, no paid API.

    1. Load & check

      Reads the CSV, checks the required columns, drops empty and duplicate reviews.

      pandas
    2. Split

      Breaks each review into sentences, and at "but" or "though", so each piece holds one opinion.

      regex
    3. Find aspects

      Matches each piece against a keyword list (the aspect taxonomy) for price, quality, delivery and support.

      taxonomy
    4. Score mood

      VADER, a lexicon-based NLP model, taught 36 shopping words, gives each piece a score from −1 to +1.

      vaderSentiment
    5. Answer

      Filters the analysed pieces for your question, groups sentences that share most of their words into recurring points, and cites the review IDs.

      FastAPI
